Typography reset

No serif display fonts. No decorative fonts. The system should feel modern, readable, and serious.

Recommended EnglishMontserrat only

Montserrat ExtraBold for headings, Montserrat Medium for UI.

This is the safest direction: consistent, modern, readable, and aligned with the existing Sky Sham brand direction. It avoids the “random font” feeling.
